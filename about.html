Our Story

Born From A Love For Slow Mornings.

How a garage roasting bench and three stubborn friends became a room where people stay for hours.

Home / Our Story
2019 — The Garage Years

Twelve Chairs, One Machine.

Brew & Bloom started as a single roasting bench in a rented garage, when three friends — a florist, a barista, and an architect — got tired of coffee that was fast but forgettable.

They wanted a room where you could hear the grinder over conversation, where the light changed the way it does in a home, and where a flower on the table wasn't decoration but part of the point. The first cafe opened on a quiet stretch of Anna Nagar with twelve chairs and one espresso machine.

2022 — Finding Our Rhythm

The Roastery Grows Up.

By the third year, the garage roaster couldn't keep up with the neighbourhood. We moved the roasting line to a proper facility, but kept sourcing the same way — direct relationships with three farms, no blends we can't explain.

The florist's instincts stayed too: every table still gets fresh stems on rotation, because a room should smell like more than just coffee.

Today

The Rule Hasn't Changed.

Every cup should feel like it was made for the person drinking it, not for a queue. That's still true whether it's your first visit or your five-hundredth.
