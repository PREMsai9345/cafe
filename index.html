Our Story

Born From A Love For Slow Mornings.

Brew & Bloom started in 2019 as a single roasting bench in a rented garage, when three friends — a florist, a barista, and an architect — got tired of coffee that was fast but forgettable.

They wanted a room where you could hear the grinder over conversation, where the light changed the way it does in a home, and where a flower on the table wasn't decoration but part of the point. The first cafe opened on a quiet stretch of Anna Nagar with twelve chairs and one espresso machine.

Today the roastery has grown, but the rule hasn't changed: every cup should feel like it was made for the person drinking it, not for a queue.
